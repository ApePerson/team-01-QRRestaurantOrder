# Restaurant manager interview

**Interviewer:** ____ · **Date and time:** ____ · **In person / phone:** ____
**Interviewed:** restaurant manager, ____ (restaurant type and area; name only with permission)
**Filed by:** Isaiah Bueno-Hernandez (elicitation lead)

Answers are recorded as the interviewer sent them, unedited.

| # | Question | Manager's answer | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do your diners mostly want to order fast, and do you have trouble with sold-out items or wrong orders? | Most customers want fast service. We sometimes have problems with sold-out items and incorrect orders, especially during busy hours. | Confirms S1, S2, S3 needs (2.2) |
| 2 | Which payment system do you use, and who pays the card fee? | We use Square for payments. The restaurant usually pays the processing fees. | Q1 answered; A5, ADR-03 |
| 3 | When a table orders a second time, is that a new ticket or added to the first? | We usually add the second order to the original ticket for the same table. | Q2 answered; CR-11 → FR-21 |
| 4 | Can the kitchen cancel one item, or only the whole order? | Yes, the kitchen can cancel one item without canceling the entire order. | Q3 answered; CR-12 → FR-22 |
| 5 | Would you want a server to confirm each QR order before the kitchen sees it? | Yes, I prefer the server to confirm the order first to avoid mistakes. | Q4 answered; CR-13 → FR-23 |
| 6 | Which languages should the menu show? | English and Spanish would be the most useful languages for our customers. | Q5 answered; CR-14 → FR-24 |
| 7 | How do you show allergens today? | We list common allergens on the menu, and customers can also ask our staff about ingredients. | Q6 answered; CR-15 → FR-25 |
| 8 | If a payment didn't go through, what should staff see? | Staff should see a clear payment failure notification so they can help the customer pay again. | Q8 answered; CR-16 → FR-26 |
| 9 | Should two phones at one table share one cart? | Yes, that would be helpful because customers at the same table can add items to one order. | Q9 answered; CR-17 → FR-27; ADR-05 reversed |
